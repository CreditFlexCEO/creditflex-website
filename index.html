Good to know

Questions? We have answers.

Clear information before you take the next step.

Is CreditFlex Academy free?

The current introductory educational course is free to explore. No account is required for the public prototype.

Can CreditFlex guarantee a higher credit score?

No. Credit outcomes depend on individual circumstances. We do not guarantee score increases or the removal of accurate negative information.

Does CreditFlex offer credit repair or dispute letters?

No. Credit repair and dispute-letter services are not currently offered. CreditFlex is focusing on general financial education.

Does this website access or store my credit reports?

No. The existing prototype does not retrieve or upload credit reports. Its sample tools may store general information in your browser. Do not enter account numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, or identity documents.

Are CreditFlex Academy courses accredited?

No. CreditFlex provides general, non-credit continuing financial education, not college degrees or accredited college credits.

Are paid memberships or personalized credit services available?

Not in the current public prototype. Secure accounts, payments, and personalized services are not enabled. The free educational preview is available now.
